What we do

Every project starts the same way: a long conversation, a short list of what matters, and a first sketch by the end of the week. We work in small numbers and finish what we begin.

Most of our work is for makers — a bakery, a bindery, two breweries and a furniture workshop that has outlasted three of our websites.
